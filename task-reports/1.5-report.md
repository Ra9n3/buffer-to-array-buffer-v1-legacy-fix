# Task 1.5 — Analog VU meter and LED segment ladder — report

**Status: APPROVED by the owner on 2026-10-30 ("approved, commit and push") after review round 1. Committed to `main` in the commit that adds this report; not tagged. (The sections below were written before the approval and are kept as written — see "Approval and commit" at the end.)**

Phase 1 — Task 1.5 — two separate reusable meter components, `VUMeter` and
`LedLadder`, shown in a new meter section of the private `#/dev/components`
playground: the VU meter at low / normal / high / overload, live on a slider
with presets and a lamp switch, enlarged lit against unlit, and at its
compact size; the ladder at low / medium / high / peak, live on a slider with
presets, and enlarged. Every value is a simulated number typed into a
fixture. No audio, analysis or project state is touched; the Console room
does not use either component.

## Summary

* **`src/components/hardware/VUMeter.svelte`** — one 120 × 84-unit SVG,
  scaled as a whole from `--vu-width` (7.5rem = 120 px at 1×). A dark walnut
  housing (the wood token sunk into the recess token, 42% at the top to 22%
  at the foot) with a bevel that is light at the top-left and shadowed at
  the bottom-right; a 0.9-unit brass bead (`amber-base 58% / metal`) with
  its own shadow line between the frame and the face; a near-black bezel
  whose lip casts down onto the card along the top, left and right; a
  cream card (the cream token — lit, yellowed 14% toward the lamp token;
  unlit, 46% of the cream over black) under a glass with a dark seam and a
  weak sheen. The scale is an arc of radius 46 about a pivot at (60, 68) —
  eleven units below the card's centre — with 29 ticks radiating from that
  pivot in three weights, numerals at −20 −10 −7 −5 −3 0 +3 only, a
  2.4-unit red band and red numerals from 0 to +3, and "VU" at the bottom
  centre of the arc. A tapered black needle (1.7 → 0.5 units) rotates about
  the pivot on a 3.2-unit hub with a blurred drop shadow offset (1.1, 1.6)
  under it; the swing is 360 ms with a slight overshoot, 120 ms under
  reduced motion; the level is clamped to the end stops.
* **The illumination** is painted, not graded: three irregular Bézier
  blobs of the incandescent token `#edb268` under Gaussian blurs of σ 13 /
  8 / 16 — a pool low and centred, a hotter core (the amber family warmed
  toward white) a little left of the axis, a faint third pool up in the
  right — over a 78%-opaque cream wash, a frosted monochrome
  `feTurbulence` grain and an off-centre vignette transparent across the
  middle. Measured on the 2.5× specimen (CIE L\*, median of a 20-unit-wide
  column): 44.0 under the bezel's lip → 69.2 at the top of the clear card →
  77 across the middle → 83.6 at the bottom centre; the brightest 6-unit
  patch is at (54.8, 73.3), left of the axis, rgb(234, 205, 164); the four
  corners are 40.4 / 39.4 / 66.4 / 70.7 (TL / TR / BL / BR); the left flank
  is 1.2 L\* brighter than the right at mid-height; the steepest change on
  clear card is 1.03 L\* per half unit — no edge anywhere. Unlit, the same
  card is a flat 43.2 across the middle. The bulb's slow drift holds
  between opacity 0.972 and 0.992 (two loops, 9.7 s and 6.3 s), and is off
  with the lamp and under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
* **`src/components/hardware/LedLadder.svelte`** — one 48-unit-wide SVG
  scaled from `--ladder-width` (3rem = 48 px at 1×), 131.5 units tall for
  the default sixteen segments. A recessed well built like the fader's
  slot; inside it sixteen 16 × 5 rounded (rx 0.6) blocks at a 7.5 pitch,
  nine green, four amber, three red from the bottom up. Lit: a HI→BASE→LO
  gradient of the band's family, a hairline specular on the top edge, a
  hard 0.5-unit black seam, a σ 1.7 bloom behind it. Unlit: the band's LO
  tone sunk into the recess with the same seam — a visible, dark lamp of
  that colour. Segments light cumulatively from the bottom by one counting
  function; dB numerals and ticks sit beside the key segments in the
  fader's convention. Nothing blurs wider than 1.7 units; no cream, no
  lamp token, no wash — it cannot be mistaken for the VU meter.
* **The playground** gains two sections, `[data-dev-component=vu-meter]`
  and `[data-dev-component=led-ladder]`, with fourteen meter instances, all
  driven by simulated numbers and labelled so on every caption and in the
  copy. The live fixtures carry a range input, presets (VU: low / normal /
  high / overload / pinned; ladder: silence / low / medium / high / peak)
  and, for the VU meter, a lamp checkbox.

The knob, the fader, the switch, their fixtures, every token, every room,
every strip and the lockfile are byte-identical to `7c1a866`. No dependency
changed. Nothing is committed.

## Component API

### `VUMeter`

| Prop | Type | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | `string \| null` | `null` | Engraved print under the housing (`L`, `R`, `MASTER`). |
| `level` | `number` | `-20` | Simulated level in VU (dB re the 0 mark). Clamped to −20…+3 for the needle; the raw value is in `aria-valuenow` within the same bounds. |
| `lamp` | `boolean` | `true` | Bulb power. |
| `lampDrift` | `boolean` | `true` | The bulb's slow brightness variation (D1.5.3). |
| `name` | `string \| null` | `null` | Accessible name when `label` is absent or terse. |

Host sizing: `--vu-width` (default 7.5rem; the height follows at 84/120).
Hooks: root `.vu[data-vu][data-vu-level][data-vu-angle][data-vu-lamp]
[data-vu-zone=normal|overload]`, classes `is-unlit` / `is-drifting` /
`is-overload`; `.vu__window[role=meter][data-vu-meter]`; every drawn part
carries `data-part=housing|trim|bezel|card|lamp|glow-pool|glow-far|
glow-core|vignette|scale|red-band|legend|needle-shadow|needle|hub|sheen`.

`vuPosition(db)` = (pct(db) − pct(−20)) / (pct(3) − pct(−20)) with
pct = 100·10^(db/20); `vuAngle(db)` = −45° + 90°·vuPosition(db).

### `LedLadder`

| Prop | Type | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `label` | `string \| null` | `null` | Engraved print under the well (`PEAK`, `PEAK L`). |
| `level` | `number` | `-60` | Simulated level in dB. Anything below the first threshold lights nothing. |
| `segments` | `LedSegment[]` | `LED_LADDER_SCALE` | Ascending thresholds with a band each; the drawing grows with the count. |
| `name` | `string \| null` | `null` | Accessible name. |

Host sizing: `--ladder-width` (default 3rem). Hooks: root
`.led[data-led][data-led-level][data-led-lit][data-led-count]
[data-led-top-band][data-led-lit-green][data-led-lit-amber][data-led-lit-red]`;
`.led__column[role=meter][data-led-meter]`; `data-part=well|segments|
segment|die|print`; each segment `[data-led-band][data-led-db]
[data-led-segment-lit]` with class `is-lit`.

`ledLitCount(segments, level)` counts up from the bottom while
`level >= threshold`, so the lit set is contiguous by construction.

## Rendering — exactly how each part is made

### VU meter — housing, trim, bezel, card

* **Housing** (`data-part=housing`): a 120 × 84 rounded rect (rx 4) filled
  with a vertical gradient `--v-housing-top` → `--v-housing-bottom`
  (`wood 42% / recess` → `wood 22% / recess`), then a second rect with the
  bevel gradient (`#fff` 16% at the top-left → 2% at 55% → `#000` 55% at
  the bottom-right, a 0°→45° linear) that gives the frame its lit top edge
  and shadowed foot, plus a 1-unit `--al-border-strong` outline.
* **Trim bead** (`data-part=trim`): a 0.9-unit stroke of `--v-trim`
  (`amber-base 58% / metal`) 3.5 units in from the housing edge, over a
  0.9-unit black-alpha stroke offset half a unit down (`vu__trim-shadow`) so
  the bead stands proud.
* **Bezel** (`data-part=bezel`): a recess-token rect between the bead and
  the card.
* **Card** (`data-part=card`): the 106 × 68 (rx 2.5) card at (7, 7), filled
  `--v-card-unlit` (`cream 46% / black`). Three "lip" gradients paint the
  bezel's shadow onto the card — 0.4 black-alpha fading over the top,
  0.3 down the left, 0.14 down the right (the light in this family comes
  from the upper left, so the right lip casts less). A 0.6-unit dark seam
  marks the glass; a faint 7%→0 white gradient from the upper left is the
  glass's sheen (`data-part=sheen`, on top of everything).

### VU meter — the incandescent illumination

Painted in the `data-part=lamp` group between the card and the print, so
the print is ink on a lit card, not ink over light:

1. **Wash** — the whole card in `--v-card-lit` (`cream 86% / lamp`) at
   fill-opacity 0.78 — the card itself lit, slightly yellowed, with the
   shadowed card showing through.
2. **Pool** (`data-part=glow-pool`) — an 83 × 46 Bézier blob of the lamp
   token centred at (58.5, 71), fill-opacity 0.66, blurred σ 13.
3. **Far pool** (`data-part=glow-far`) — a 48 × 38 blob centred at (84, 41),
   up in the right, opacity 0.2, blurred σ 16: the irregularity in the
   falloff.
4. **Core** (`data-part=glow-core`) — a 39 × 32 blob of `--v-lamp-core`
   (`amber-hi 55% / white`) centred at (57.5, 74), 2.5 units left of the
   axis and almost on the card's lower edge, opacity 0.6, blurred σ 8: the
   bulb.
5. **Grain** — `feTurbulence fractalNoise` base 0.9, two octaves,
   desaturated, at opacity 0.045: the card's frosting.
6. **Vignette** (`data-part=vignette`) — a radial gradient centred (0.5,
   0.7) with its focus at (0.46, 0.76), transparent to 46%, 0.14 black at
   76%, 0.4 at the edge: dark toward the far corners only.

The pool, far pool and core are clipped to the card; the blurs' margins
are inside the filter regions. The `lamp` prop fades the whole group's
opacity to 0 over 480 ms (back on over 360 ms); the drift is two CSS
keyframe loops on the `.vu__bulb` and `.vu__bulb-core` groups.

### VU meter — scale, needle, hub

* **Arc and ticks** (`data-part=scale`): `arc(r, fromDb, toDb)` builds an
  SVG `A` path about the pivot; `polar(r, angle)` places every tick's two
  ends on radii from the pivot, so each tick points at the hub. 29 ticks in
  three weights (major 5, medium 3.6, minor 2.3 units; strokes 0.9 / 0.7 /
  0.5) at the marks of `VU_MARKS` and the halves between them; the arc is
  0.8 thick in `--v-ink` (`--al-text-on-cream`).
* **Numerals** at radius 55.5 for the seven numeral marks only, 6.6 px
  bold condensed, black ink; the `0` and `+3` are `--v-red`.
* **Red band** (`data-part=red-band`): the arc from 0 to +3 at radius
  46 + 0.35 + 1.2, stroke 2.4, `--v-red` (`red-base 85% / red-lo`).
* **"VU"** (`data-part=legend`): 9 px black-weight text at (60, 52.5),
  bottom centre of the arc, above the hub.
* **Needle** (`data-part=needle`): a path from 2.5 units behind the pivot
  to radius 53, 1.7 wide at the base tapering to 0.5, `--v-needle`
  (`text-on-cream 55% / black`); `transform: rotate(var(--vu-angle))` with
  `transform-box: view-box; transform-origin: var(--vu-pivot)` where
  `--vu-pivot` is `50% 80.95%` (60/120, 68/84) — measured in Chromium as
  `60px 67.998px` on the 1× specimen. Transition 360 ms
  `cubic-bezier(.22, 1.16, .4, 1)`; 120 ms ease-out under reduced motion.
* **Shadow** (`data-part=needle-shadow`): the same path, `#000` at 0.32,
  blurred σ 0.9, `translate(1.1, 1.6) rotate(angle)`, painted before the
  needle — the blade's shadow sits down and to the right on the card.
* **Hub** (`data-part=hub`): a 3.2-unit disc of `--v-hub`
  (`metal-shadow 45% / black`) with a lighter rim and a 1.1 pin, over the
  needle's tail.

The tip at radius 53 clears the card's edges at both stops (the right stop
reaches x 97.5, y 30.5 inside the 7…113 × 7…75 card).

### LED ladder — well and segments

* **Well** (`data-part=well`): a recess-token rect at (4, 4), 22 wide, with
  a dark top lip, a lit lower edge and a hairline of the strong border —
  the fader slot's construction.
* **Segment** (`data-part=segment`, one `<g>` per threshold, bottom first):
  a `die` rect 16 × 5 rx 0.6 filled either `url(#uid-lit-{band})` — a
  vertical gradient `--l-{band}-top` (`hi 70% / base`) → `-mid`
  (`hi 22% / base`) → `-bottom` (`base 78% / lo`) — or `--l-{band}-off`
  (`lo 30% / recess`); a 0.5 `#000` seam on every die; a `lens` hairline
  (`#fff` 0.35) along the top edge when lit; a `bloom` rect behind the die,
  the band's BASE tone blurred σ 1.7, opacity 0.75 when lit and 0 when not,
  transition 60 ms. No transform transition, no easing — an LED is
  instant.
* **Print** (`data-part=print`): 1.8-unit ticks and 6.4 px mono numerals
  (−40 −20 −10 −6 −3 0 +4) three units clear of the ticks, right of the
  well.

Measured on `evidence/led-25x.png` (120 × 330 px): lit green dies
rgb(110, 172, 127) L\* 65, lit amber rgb(232, 183, 119) L\* 77, unlit red
rgb(56, 29, 24) L\* 14, the well floor rgb(14, 13, 12); the gap above the
top lit segment carries its bloom, rgb(91, 69, 42). The top edge of the top
lit segment goes from L\* 24.7 to 58.1 to 83.7 in two 2.5×-pixel rows
(0.8 unit) — a hard seam, with the bloom a soft 1–2-unit skirt outside
it.

## Accessibility

* Both components are **outputs**: `role="meter"` with `aria-valuemin`,
  `aria-valuemax`, `aria-valuenow` and a spoken `aria-valuetext`; the SVG is
  `aria-hidden`. VU: "−7 VU", "0 VU, in the red", "−20 VU, lamp off".
  Ladder: "−3 dB, 11 of 16 segments lit, amber". The name is `name ??
  label ?? "VU meter"` / `"level ladder"`.
* No pointer or key handlers, no `tabindex`, no buttons inside either
  component (`check:meter` asserts it). The interactive controls belong to
  the fixtures: a native `<input type=range>` with a visible label
  ("Simulated level (demo only)") and a live readout, native buttons for
  the presets, a native checkbox for the lamp ("Lamp (simulated power)").
* `prefers-reduced-motion`: the needle's 360 ms swing becomes 120 ms, the
  bulb's drift and the lamp fades stop. The ladder has no motion beyond a
  60 ms opacity step.
* The lit/unlit distinction in the ladder is not colour alone: every unlit
  die is darker than the well's border and every lit one carries a
  highlight and a bloom; the lit count is in the text.
* Print contrast: the ink is L\* 16; the lit card beside the numerals is
  L\* 59–70 (the −5 / −3 / 0 at the top of the arc sit in the bezel's lip
  shadow, 59–63; the rest 68–70), roughly 4.6:1 at the darkest; the unlit
  card is 34–41 beside them, about 2:1 — legible, as the spec asks, but
  the unlit meter is a shadowed instrument, not a reading surface.

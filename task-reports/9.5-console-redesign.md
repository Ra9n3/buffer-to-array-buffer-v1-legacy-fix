# Task 9.5 — The one-window Console redesign

**Owner instruction of 2026-11-12**, with a reference photograph of a vintage 12-channel console:
redesign the Console tab to fit one window, following the photo's layout — "what concern us is the
layout itself and the sizes of the channels and the upper meters." Six questions were asked and
answered first (branch, transport placement, strip geometry, overflow behaviour, meter contents,
MIX's place); every decision is recorded in `decisions-log.md` as **D9.5.1–D9.5.5**.

Based on `arena/45794886` @ b988358 (the newest work: the 9.2/9.3 hardware chrome plus the
reverted 9.4 channel-bank attempt). The 9.4 finding stands — the bank was handed 446 px for
600 px strips — but its remedy (delete the transport deck) is superseded by the reference's own
proportions, which fit all three zones.

## 2. Measured, not asserted

At the owner's **1920×1080**: layout `scrollHeight == clientHeight` (no vertical scroll), bridge
134 px, bank 768 px (strips stretch to 752 px, the fader section absorbing +180 px), transport
99 px with its bottom edge 8 px above the viewport's, 8 whole track strips + 3 Returns + the bus
in the first screenful. At **1600×900**: the same test passes with zero slack to spare — the
compact geometry was tuned for exactly this (three size iterations, measured in the running app).
Below ~870 px of window height the room scrolls internally rather than crushing the strips.

Verified in a real browser against the running dev server (geometry assertions via the console,
plus four VLM review passes of full-page screenshots; every defect those passes found — the dead
band under a non-stretching bank, the non-spanning meter column, the truncated knob legends, the
colliding spectrum numerals — was fixed and re-photographed).

## 5. Known, deliberate limitations

- **A partial strip at the scroller's fold.** The tracks and Returns rails snap to whole modules
  and fade at the edge; the first screenful can still end mid-strip (the reference's console is
  simply built exactly as wide as its 12 channels — a browser window is not). This is the
  standard DAW bank behaviour and reads as a scroller's edge, not a broken module.
- **The strip meters are fixtures**, like every reading on the bridge: no audio, no analysis
  (plan §14 Phases 5 and 8 wire the real metering; `StripMeter`'s `level`/`peak` props are the
  seam).
- **The bus master's blank panel** (D2.4.4) still carries no "no sends" print — it reads as an
  intentional blank in review, and the recorded decision stands.
- **Windows shorter than ~870 px** scroll the room vertically rather than shrinking any approved
  component; the primary targets are 1080p (the owner's screen) and 900p, both of which fit
  exactly.

Phase 2 — Task 2.3 — the visual design of the Console room's
transport / master-control area in the approved hardware language, on
fixture data only: a full-width transport deck between the approved meter
bridge and the approved channel bank, carrying four transport keys
(PREVIOUS · PLAY · STOP · NEXT — the approved `TactileSwitch`), a lamp-lit
time window, a lamp-lit status window and the dominant illuminated MIX
button. Nothing is wired: no playback, recording, locating, mixing,
rendering, sync, position tracking, timers, audio or project state. The
deck's only behaviour is the keys' own local latch / release and the MIX
cap's press feedback.

## Summary

- **Placement (owner, D2.3.1).** The plan's "upper section" is filled by
  the approved bridge, whose reserved panel is 53 px wide at 1280 and absent
  below; rather than redesign the bridge or shrink approved components, the
  builder asked and the owner chose a **third Console zone**: a full-width
  deck between bridge and bank, wearing the bridge's housing recipe exactly
  as the bank does. Same layout at every width; the bridge is untouched.
- **Transport keys (owner, D2.3.2).** Exactly the Phase 0 four, each the
  approved cap-labelled switch at its approved 40-px size, symbol on the
  cap (|◀ ▶ ■ ▶|), word engraved beneath. PLAY / STOP latch and exclude
  each other (the engaged key's own lamp is lit); PREVIOUS / NEXT are
  momentary. No record, loop or mode keys; no source selector — the
  listening source (plan §6 RAW / PREVIEW / RENDER) is printed as text in
  the status window.
- **Time display (owner, D2.3.3).** Dark glass, amber lamp-lit monospaced
  tabular digits: `H:MM:SS` large with no leading hour zero (`0:02:17`),
  `BAR 12 · BEAT 3` small beneath. Glass breathes 4 s like the family's
  lamps; still under reduced motion.
- **Status display (plan §3, §10, §11).** Same glass: the state word first
  (READY / the processing stage name / MIXED), then dot-separated details —
  source, hardware profile (LITE / BALANCED / PERFORMANCE), the estimate;
  while processing, `Stage 3 of 7 · 42% · 0:58 left · LITE · 1 warning`
  with a lamp-lit progress groove and the warning in the red family. No
  spinner.
- **MIX (plan §3 "large illuminated", §10).** Its own component, unlike any
  key: a 120 × 56 px lit amber rectangular lens in a dark well with a warm
  spill on the panel, "MIX" printed dark on the lens, "AUTOMATIC MIX"
  engraved beneath, at the right end of the deck. Stands 3 px proud, sinks
  under the finger, visible focus ring. States via fixtures: ready (steady),
  processing (slow pulse, `aria-disabled`), complete (steady). **No click
  handler** — pressing it starts nothing.
- **Default (owner, D2.3.4).** The room shows READY; PLAYING, PROCESSING and
  COMPLETE decks and all MIX states (plus an unlit cap) are in the
  playground section "Console — Transport deck and MIX button".
- **Responsive.** One row at 1920 / 1280 / 1024 (deck 125.1 px), two rows at
  768 (keys + time, then status + MIX; deck 208.9 px); no clipping, no
  overlap, no horizontal overflow, no component shrunk (measured below).
- **Verification.** svelte-check 0 / 0; all twelve guards pass including the

## Rendering — how each requirement is met

### Transport controls (reuse approved tactile components; hardware styling; states via fixtures; labels or recognisable symbols)
The four keys are the approved Task 1.4 `TactileSwitch` in its cap-labelled
variant, bound through its public `engaged` / `onToggle` API only, at the
size it was approved at (`--switch-size: 2.5rem` → 40 × 40 px caps,
measured). The cap prints the conventional symbol (glyph boxes measured
16.1 / 11.5 / 9.2 / 16.1 × 14.3 px at 1920) and the word is engraved
beneath, centred under the cap (the switch's lamp and its gap are left out
of the centring). State: the engaged key's own built-in lamp (amber = active,
D1.6) — STOP lit in READY, PLAY lit in PLAYING. The latch / release logic
is the deck's own local UI state seeded from the fixture; verified in the
browser (below). No playback, recording or engine.

### Status display (representative fixture values, clear hierarchy, industrial look, legible at all widths)
`LampDisplay kind="status"`: engraved STATUS legend over dark layered glass;
the state word in the label face at 1.125 rem, uppercase, lamp-lit with a
soft glow; the details one step smaller and dimmer, dot-separated, wrapping
to a second line where the window is narrow (1024: `SOURCE RAW · BALANCED ·`
/ `EST. MIX 1:40` — measured no overflow); the warning part in the red
family; the progress groove (4 px, lamp fill at 42 %) only while
processing. Window: 448 × 90.5 px at 1920 / 1280 / 768, 256.9 × 90.5 at
1024 (its 16-rem floor). Status text is never clipped (scrollWidth ≤
clientWidth on every text part at all four widths).

### Time display (format per owner; no sync / position tracking)
`LampDisplay kind="clock"`: `0:02:17` in `--al-font-mono` 1.75 rem (28 px
measured), tabular, letter-spaced, lamp-lit; `BAR 12 · BEAT 3` beneath.
Window 184 × 90.5 px. `formatClock` never pads the hour; minutes and seconds
are two digits. The values are fixture numbers; nothing advances them.

### MIX (large, illuminated, visually dominant, unmistakably different from Play)
At 1920 the MIX cap is 120 × 56 px (4.2 × the area of a key cap) in a
136 × 75 px housing, the only lit lens on the deck, the only warm spill on
the panel, the only rectangular control, at the far right with
`margin-left: auto` so it reads as the destination of the row. Play is a
small cream square key with a symbol; MIX is a large amber glowing lens
with a word. States (fixtures): `ready` lit steady; `processing` 1.8-s
pulse of the lens and spill, `aria-disabled="true"` (Playwright itself
refused to click it as "not enabled"; a forced click changed nothing);
`complete` lit steady. No mixing: no handler at all.

### Materials
No hex literal in any transport file (guarded); the lamp colour is
`--al-material-lamp` and the amber family; warnings `--al-function-red`;
the housing is the bridge recipe from the wood / metal / panel tokens; the
glass and lens are layered gradients with inset shadows (spec §6, never
flat). No cold VFD colouring (guarded: no blue / cyan / teal / green / LED
words in the deck's CSS). No screw heads anywhere (owner's standing rule;
guarded).

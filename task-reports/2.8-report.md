## Summary

- **What the panel shows for a standard track** (top to bottom):
  1. **Identity header** from the shared session — code, flat org tab,
     name, family · source print (the Arrange data), Role, Tier, "Standard
     track". Same walnut cheeks + charcoal deck as the bank (values copied
     from `MixerBank.svelte`).
  2. **Processing chain** readout — the plan's eight blocks (§8) in order,
     `01 Input trim / gain … 08 Sends + bus routing`, each with an amber
     lamp: lit = in circuit, dark = bypassed. The lamps follow the fixture
     state and the two bypass switches (EQ bypass darkens 03 and 04, the
     compressor bypass darkens 05; 02 is dark on tracks whose fixture has
     no cleanup/expander). Tooltips say where a block's controls live
     (strip / this panel / no controls in this build). Nothing can be
     reordered or routed.
  3. **EQ bay** (amber family): BYPASS switch, AUTO / MANUAL controls, the
     **response graph** (log 20 Hz – 20 kHz, ±15 dB; solid amber = the
     current curve computed from the control values; dotted cream = the
     curve the track loaded with, drawn only once something changed;
     hatched area under a dynamic band = its illustrative attenuation
     range; band markers LF / LMF / HMF / HF), then four band plates — LF
     low shelf (FREQ, GAIN), LMF and HMF bells (FREQ, GAIN, Q), HF high
     shelf (FREQ, GAIN). Every knob is the approved Knob at the strips'
     40 px, with a value window, and a **MODE** switch (Auto / Manual) of
     its own. A band that carries the dynamic-EQ block is marked **DYN**.
  4. **Compressor bay** (red family): BYPASS, AUTO / MANUAL, **CHARACTER**
     (approved ScribbleSelect: VCA · Opto · FET · Vari-Mu) with its own
     MODE switch and a "was <X>" ghost print, the five knobs THRESH ·
     RATIO · ATTACK · RELEASE · MAKEUP, and the **GAIN REDUCTION** meter —
     the approved VU meter housing with a dedicated **GR face** (0 at the
     right, 20 at the left, numerals 0 1 3 5 10 20, no red band, "GR"
     legend), a fixed fixture reading, 0 while bypassed.
  5. **What changed and why** — a cream card of two to four authored
     sentences per track, each tagged EQ / DYN EQ / COMP / CLEANUP, stamped
     **FIXTURE TEXT**; the tooltip says no audio was analysed and no engine
     has run.
- **Auto / Manual = ownership** (owner's clarification): turning a knob
  makes **only that parameter** Manual (lamp lit) and shows the ghost of
  its loaded value; its MODE switch returns it to Auto **changing ownership
  only** — the value stays, nothing is reset or suggested. Each module
  prints **AUTO / MIXED / MANUAL** in an engraved window and has **ALL
  Auto** / **ALL Manual** switches whose lamps are lit only when the whole
  module is theirs (never a binary lamp for a Mixed module). Bypass is a
  separate state.
- **Ghosts (plan §10):** a dashed pointer at the loaded value, drawn in an
  overlay outside the Knob's own artwork (Knob.svelte untouched); the
  dotted original curve; "was Opto" under the character.
- **Per-track state:** one chain per standard track, created from its
  fixture when the room mounts and edited in place; leaving a track and
  coming back shows its edits; tracks never leak into each other. Nothing
  is persisted (room lifetime only, by design).
- **Non-standard channels:** a Return or a bus shows the identity header
  and "Processing detail for return bus / bus channels is not available in
  this build" — never a standard-track chain, never a blank bay. No
  selection shows "Select a channel in the bank to inspect it".
- **Layout:** full-width panel below the bank, unchanged bank geometry at
  every width (160 × 755.5 strips, 81.1 px identity, rails and pinned block
  identical to the 2.7 matrix). EQ and compressor bays sit **side by side
  from 1280 px up** (the EQ bay takes the remaining width; the compressor
  bay is its content's 568 px) and **stack below 1280 px**; the four band
  plates fold to two rows when the EQ bay is narrower than 632 px (only at
  1280). No knob is ever shrunk; no horizontal overflow anywhere.
- **Shared-component change (one, backward-compatible):** `VUMeter` gains
  an optional `face` prop (`"vu"` default — unchanged — or `"gr"`). The
  Console fingerprint is byte-for-byte the 2.7 baseline.

## The chain and the fixture states

**Blocks (plan §8), from `PROCESSING_CHAIN_STEPS`:** 01 Input trim / gain
(strip) · 02 Cleanup / expander (fixture state only; no controls in this
build) · 03 Static multiband EQ (panel) · 04 Dynamic EQ (panel; the DYN
band) · 05 Compressor (panel) · 06 Panning (strip) · 07 Channel fader
(strip) · 08 Sends + bus routing (strip).

**EQ parameters:** FREQ 20 Hz – 20 kHz (log; prints `60 Hz`, `3.5 kHz`,
`10 kHz`), GAIN −15 … +15 dB in 0.5 dB (prints `+2.5 dB`, `−3.0 dB`), Q
0.3 – 10 (log; bells only). **Compressor:** THRESH −40 … 0 dB in 1 dB,
RATIO 1 · 1.5 · 2 · 3 · 4 · 6 · 8 · 10 · 20 : 1 (list), ATTACK 0.1 – 100 ms
(log), RELEASE 10 – 1000 ms (log), MAKEUP 0 … +20 dB in 0.5 dB. Keyboard
and pointer on the Knob move the position; the chain converts position ↔
value by taper and snaps to step / list.

**Fixture coverage (20 tracks):** every track has a four-band setting, a
character, five compressor values, a fixed GR reading and 2–4 notes.
Representative states — **dynamic bands** (shaded range + DYN mark) on
01 (LMF, 3 dB), 04 (HMF 3), 08 (LF 2), 10 / 11 (HMF 3), 13 (LMF 2), 14
(HMF 4), 17 / 18 (HMF 3); **already-Manual parameters with a loaded value
to ghost**: 01 HMF GAIN (+3.0, loaded +2.0), 08 LF GAIN (+2.0, loaded
+1.0), 12 HMF FREQ (3 kHz, loaded 2.5 kHz), 15 THRESH (−22, loaded −20);
**changed character**: 02 (VCA, was Opto); **EQ bypassed**: 20 Riser
(graph dimmed, blocks 03 / 04 dark); **compressor bypassed**: 04 Hi-hat
and 19 Shaker (GR 0, block 05 dark); **cleanup / expander in circuit**
(block 02 lit) on 01, 02, 03, 09, 15, 16, 17, 18 and absent elsewhere
(02 dark); GR readings from 0 to 9 dB (07 Room, crushed on purpose);
characters: VCA × 10, Opto × 5, FET × 2, Vari-Mu × 3. The module status
therefore loads as MIXED on 01 / 08 / 12 (EQ) and 02 / 15 (compressor),
AUTO elsewhere.

**Explanation text:** authored per track in the plan's register ("Reduced
energy around 3 kHz, up to 3 dB, to make room for the lead vocal when it
sings"); stamped FIXTURE TEXT; the stamp's tooltip: "Authored fixture
text for <track>. No audio has been analysed and no engine has run; real
explanations come from engine decisions in a later phase." The card does
not update when a knob is turned (there is no engine to explain a change);
the knob's own tooltip carries "loaded as <value>".

## Assumptions — where the documents are silent (please correct any)

1. **Band count and shape.** The plan says "static multiband EQ" and
   "EQ curve" without a count. Built: four bands — LF low shelf, LMF and
   HMF bells (with Q), HF high shelf — the console form the hardware
   reference's layout implies. Any other count or shape is a fixture /
   spec change, not a layout change.
2. **Compressor controls.** The plan names only the character (VCA /
   Opto / FET / Vari-Mu) and the gain-reduction display. Built: threshold,
   ratio, attack, release, make-up — the conventional set. Ranges and
   tapers as listed above.
3. **Graph window** ±15 dB (the gain knobs' whole range), log 20 Hz –
   20 kHz, shelf / bell maths used **only to draw** the curve from the
   control values (plan §3 "EQ curve"; owner: "consistent with local
   control values, not a spectrum").
4. **GR scale** 0 – 20 dB with numerals 0 1 3 5 10 20; fixed readings per
   track (owner: "dedicated gain-reduction scale … fixed illustrative
   values").
5. **Ghost pointer** is drawn only once a value differs from the loaded
   one (plan §10 "alongside the current result"); it stays after the
   parameter returns to Auto because the value still differs.
6. **Dynamic EQ** has no controls of its own: the DYN mark and the shaded
   range are the fixture's illustrative depth (owner: "shading =
   illustrative attenuation range only; no extra controls").
7. **The chain readout** is a lamp rail of the eight blocks; the blocks
   whose controls are on the strip (trim, pan, fader, sends) are not
   duplicated in the panel (plan: "complete processing chain" read as the
   readout, the strip remaining the primary surface, prompt).
8. **Cleanup / expander** is shown as a block state only (lamp + note).
   The plan gives it no controls; none were invented.
9. **Switch and print lettering:** MODE / BYPASS / ALL / AUTO · MANUAL /
   MIXED and the knob legends FREQ · GAIN · Q · THRESH · RATIO · ATTACK ·
   RELEASE · MAKEUP; the explanation's module tags EQ / DYN EQ / COMP /
   CLEANUP; the stamp "FIXTURE TEXT". All re-letterable.
10. **Header facts** Role and Tier are printed from the fixture (the
    Arrange data); the Tier is the fixture's value until the shared
    tier state arrives in Task 3.1 (2.7 follow-up).
11. **Layout thresholds:** EQ bay `flex: 1 1 36rem`, compressor bay its
    content width (568 px) → side by side from 1280 up; band fold under a
    632 px EQ bay (container query `39.5rem`); plates are their module's
    height rather than stretched to a common height.

## Deviations, issues and follow-ups

- **Placeholder regions dropped from the `check:mixer` list:** the Task
  0.5 placeholder tagged `sends`, `auto-manual` and `ghost-indicator`
  regions. The 2.8 panel has no sends section (sends live on the strips
  and routing is out of scope); Auto / Manual and ghosts are per module /
  per knob (`AutoManualControls`, `[data-chain-ghost]`) and are checked by
  the new guard. The plan's §3 list does name "sends" among the Mixer
  contents — they are on every strip; a sends view inside the detail
  would be a separate decision.
- **Deck under the compressor plate.** At ≥ 1280 the compressor plate
  (260.6 px) is shorter than the EQ plate (448 / 638 px) and the charcoal
  deck shows beneath it; plates are deliberately not stretched over
  nothing. If the owner prefers equal-height plates, it is one
  declaration (`align-items: stretch`).
- **Band fold at exactly 1280** (EQ bay 623.7 px < 632): the four plates
  go to two rows and the panel is 871 px. The alternative — stacking the
  bays at 1280 — makes the panel taller still (954 px); neither shrinks
  a control.
- **Panel below the fold** at 1000 px-tall windows (2.7 accepted
  behaviour): the room scrolls; the panel top is at 283 (1440) / 298
  (1920) / 75 (1280) after scrolling, and at 1024 / 768 the 954 px panel
  is taller than the viewport, so the room scrolls within it.
- **Knob numeric entry on log / list tapers:** the approved Knob's typed
  entry works in knob positions (0–1000 for log / list parameters, dB for
  linear ones). The chain prints the real value in the window; typing a
  frequency into the knob is not supported in this build (Knob is not to
  be changed; a `parse` prop would be the clean follow-up).
- **The explanation card is static fixture text** — it does not react to
  edits (no engine); the knob tooltips carry the "loaded as" fact.
- **State lives for the room's lifetime only** (by design, no
  persistence); navigating to another room and back resets edits.
- Carried forward unchanged: the push of the four local commits; the 2.7
  follow-ups (shared tier / selection state → 3.1, numeric Priority
  override, Return / bus identity wording); the Return / bus detail (2.9);
  `-∞` fader bottom; Knob Shift-fine step; switch-lamp migration; README.

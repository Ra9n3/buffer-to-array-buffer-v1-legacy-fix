## 3. The VU meter's `fill` mode, precisely

The owner: "the vu meters should be extended with their content." The meter's whole
drawing is one SVG at 1 px = 1 unit; the `fill` mode re-cuts its width to the window's
measured box (`SW = winW · 84 / winH`, so the viewBox's shape IS the box's shape — an
exact fit, never letterboxed, never distorted):

- the housing, bezel and card rects run the full stretched width;
- the pivot — and with it the hub, the "VU" print and the needle — rides the card's
  centre line (the 1× pivot translated by half the extra width);
- the scale spreads: the sweep widens 90° → up to 114° (±57°, capped), the arc, ticks and
  numerals keeping their approved 1× radii and spacing so the print never crowds the
  card's fixed height;
- the needle's position law — linear in modulation, clamped to the end stops — is
  untouched; only its angular spread widens;
- the bulb's three blurred blobs ride the card's centre line (a translate; their drawn
  geometry unchanged).

At the default box the drawing is the approved face, value for value — the meter guard's
every 1×-face assertion still passes, the pins re-cut with this task's note.

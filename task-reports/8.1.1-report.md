# Task 8.1.1 — Console-only layout correction

**Status:** complete, uncommitted, awaiting review.
**Branch:** `arena/666d8683-buffer-to-array-buffer-v1-lega`.
**Scope:** the Console room's layout only. No Mixer change, no Arrange change, no Home
or Settings change, no component, token, DSP, engine, playback, project-schema or
dependency change.

## 1. What was wrong

`4c5ec57` ("fix(ui): restore approved Console and Mixer layouts") added a fourth zone
to the Console — a selected-channel processing detail panel over the channel bank —
and changed the room grid from

```css
grid-template-rows: max-content max-content minmax(max-content, 1fr);
```

to two fractional rows below the fixed bridge and deck:

```css
grid-template-rows: max-content max-content minmax(0, 0.95fr) minmax(0, 1.05fr);
```

That detail panel is the **Mixer's** surface. The plan (§3) gives the Console a meter
bridge, a transport and channel strips — "the lower section contains channel strips",
"the upper section is a vintage meter bridge" — and gives processing detail, EQ curves,
compressor and gain-reduction displays to the Mixer alone. The Console's strips also
deliberately exclude the Mixer's tier and instrument controls.

Because `minmax(0, …)` lets a grid row collapse below its content and the room's
overflow is `hidden`, the two fractional rows could not scroll. Measured in a real
browser at 100 % zoom on the build of `4c5ec57`:

| viewport | detail zone | bank zone (visible) | bank housing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1366 × 768 | 30.2 px | 33.4 px | 719 px |
| 1920 × 1080 | 178.4 px | 197.2 px | 719 px |

At 1366 × 768 the Console showed **49 px of its 719 px mixer** — a single sliver of the
KICK strip, with the channel bank's own legend cut in half. The panel that displaced it
was itself unusable at 30 px. The "one-screen" arrangement showed neither surface.

## 2. What was changed

1. **`src/rooms/ConsoleRoom.svelte`** — restored to the approved file, byte for byte
   (sha256 `c937b69e2d4c6c2a…`, the hash five guards already pin as approved). The
   Console is the bridge, the transport deck and the bank, in that order, on the
   approved grid `max-content / max-content / minmax(max-content, 1fr)` with
   `overflow-y: auto` inside the bounded room. The detail zone, its
   `MixerDetailPanel` import, the room's `selectedId` / `selectedChannel` state and
   the bank's click/focus selection handlers are gone with it.
2. **Guards restored** to their pre-correction contracts, so the approved Console is
   pinned again rather than the four-zone shape:
   - `check-console-bridge`, `check-console-transport`, `check-transport` and
     `check-channel-strip` are back to their three-zone expectations.
   - The `ConsoleRoom.svelte` hash pin is back to `c937b69e2d4c6c2a…` in
     `check-arrange-rows`, `check-arrange-timeline`, `check-bus-return`,
     `check-home-settings` and `check-mixer`.
   - `check-label-lamp`'s scope list regains `ConsoleRoom.svelte`. The correction
     exemption had removed it on the grounds that "their skinned faces print their
     legends with EngravedLabel"; the Console prints no legend of its own — its three
     zone headings belong to the bridge, the deck and the bank — so the exemption was
     not justified and is withdrawn. `MixerRoom.svelte` and `MixerChannelStrip.svelte`
     stay exempt, as the Mixer work requires.
3. **Three guards added** to `check-console-bridge-structure.mjs` (90 → 93 checks) so
   this specific regression cannot return silently: the Console carries no
   `channel-detail` zone and imports no `MixerDetailPanel`; it keeps no selection state
   of its own; and the bank's row keeps its `minmax(max-content, 1fr)` content floor so
   nothing above can crush the strips again.

Nothing else in the tree changed. The Mixer keeps the two-column layout, the compact
selectable channel headers and the detail panel that `4c5ec57` gave it, exactly as it
was; the Arrange, Home, Settings and playground routes are untouched.

## 5. Deviations and notes

- **The bank is deeper than the 1366 × 768 viewport allows.** This is inherent in the
  approved components and was measured on the approved build too: the bank is a fixed
  719 px housing plus a 160 px strip module, and the bridge (293 px) and deck (125 px)
  above it are fixed physical hardware that the standing rules forbid shrinking. Below
  a ~1 200 px window the approved Console therefore scrolls vertically inside the room
  — by design, "never the page" (D2.1.x, D2.1.8) — showing the bank's legend and the top
  ~49 px of the strips at 1366 × 768, and 179 px at 1920 × 1080. The correction restores
  exactly that approved behaviour; it does not attempt to make the desk fit where the
  approved desk never fitted.
- **If the intent is a Console that shows its strips without scrolling at 768 px**, that
  is a design change, not a layout fix, and it needs an owner decision. The only levers
  are the ones the standing rules protect: shrinking the bridge, the deck or the strips,
  or collapsing those fixed decks. Worth putting to the owner as a separate task with
  the numbers above.
- The room header band (67 px here) and the shell's mini transport (44 px) are unchanged
  shell furniture, counted in the measurements above as they ship.

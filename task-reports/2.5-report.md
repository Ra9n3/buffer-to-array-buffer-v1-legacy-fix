## Rendering — how each requirement is met

- **Track name + instrument identity**: name bright on line 1; instrument
  in the window on line 2, uppercase mono as every value window in the
  family ("ELECTRIC GUITAR", "ROOM MIC", "FX").
- **Org colour as a restrained marker**: the 6 × 16 flat tab only;
  `--org` is read by nothing else in the row; the face is the panel colour
  (`rgb(35, 33, 29)` measured) — never a tint. Guarded.
- **Role label, distinct from Tier**: role = engraved print at the left of
  line 3 (RHYTHM / LEAD / DOUBLE / HARMONY; tooltip "Role: Stacked / double
  — the track's musical function"); tier = the 88 px window at the right
  (PRIMARY / SUPPORTING / BACKGROUND; tooltip "Prominence tier, Snare
  Bottom: Supporting — how the track is treated in mix conflicts"). The
  corner's ROLE and TIER legends sit over the two columns. Role is not
  editable in this task (plan §5 lists it as metadata; the prompt asks for
  a role *label*).
- **Prominence Tier control**: the xs ScribbleSelect; three options;
  default Supporting for an unset track.
- **Instrument combobox**: the sm ScribbleSelect; 19 fixture instruments;
  the window prints the current one; full keyboard; the plate scrolls.
- **Compact and readable**: three lines in 80 px; 11 rows visible at 1080
  tall at every width (unchanged from Phase 0's single-line rows).
- **Concise labels / tooltips, no decorative text**: four legends in the
  corner, two tooltips per row plus the truncated name's title and the
  code's "Track 01"; nothing else.
- **Fixture-only, local state**: D2.5.6, D2.5.7; guarded.

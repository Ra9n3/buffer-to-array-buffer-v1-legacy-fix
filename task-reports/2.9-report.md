## Summary

**What the owner sees.** In the Mixer, pressing SEL on **R1 Room Reverb**
turns the panel below the bank into the Return's detail: the identity
header (R1 · ROOM REVERB · "Reverb · Room" · Input 8 sends · Chain In
circuit · Return bus); a five-block rail — **01 Sends in · 02 Reverb · 03
Tone / damping · 04 Return level · 05 Output → Master**; a **Sends in**
plate listing the eight tracks that send to it (05 Overhead L −30 dB …
19 Shaker −26 dB, in the green send print); the **Reverb** effect module
— CHARACTER (Room ▾) with MODE, three **green** knobs **DECAY 1.4 s ·
PRE-DELAY 12 ms · TONE −2** each with MODE, the module's AUTO / MIXED /
MANUAL window with ALL AUTO · ALL MANUAL, and BYPASS; and the authored
"What changed and why" card (EFFECT / TONE / OUTPUT). **R3 Delay 1**
shows TIME 375 ms · FEEDBACK 35 % · TONE −4, TIME already Manual (loaded
350 ms, ghost drawn), the chain bypassed: blocks 02 and 03 dark, BYPASS
lit, header "Chain Bypassed". **B1 Mix Bus** shows the bus's seven-block
rail — **01 Bus trim · 02 Static multiband EQ · 03 Compressor · 04 Balance
· 05 Bus fader · 06 Output → Master · 07 Peak protection** (the last a dark
lamp: "not engaged (the mix leaves headroom)") — the same EQ graph + four
bands and compressor + GR meter a track has, with gentle bus fixtures
(300 Hz −1 dB, 12 kHz +1 dB; Vari-Mu 1.5:1, 1 dB of reduction), and four
notes including the peak-protection / no-mastering note.

**One state per Return, shared.** Turn DECAY on the Mixer's R1 strip and
the panel's DECAY moves with it, becomes Manual and draws its ghost; turn
PRE-DELAY in the panel and the strip's knob follows; choose **Hall** in
the panel and the strip's character window prints **HALL** (and the
identity tooltip, the header and the "was Room" ghost follow); press
BYPASS on the strip and the panel's module, rail and header read
bypassed — and back from the panel. The Console's R1 strip is untouched
by any of it (measured: ROOM · 1.4 s · 12 ms · in circuit after the
Mixer's R1 had been changed to Hall · 1.6 s · 15 ms).

**Switching never leaves stale controls.** track → Return → bus → track →
Return were cycled under measurement: a Return shows exactly one chain
module (`effect`) and the sends-in list and no EQ / compressor / GR /
graph; the bus shows exactly `eq` + `comp`, no effect module, no sends-in
list, no dynamic band, no cleanup; a track shows the 2.8 eight-block rail.

### Where the documents are silent (assumptions — please correct any)

1. **Return plate widths.** The effect module and the sends list are their
   content's width (like the 2.8 compressor plate: "a plate is never
   stretched over panel it has nothing to put on"); the deck shows to
   the right at wide widths. They sit side by side from 1024 px and
   stack at 768 px.
2. **Which sends feed a Return** is read from the track *fixtures'* send
   slots (`TRACK_STRIPS[].sends`), not from the strips' live send-slot
   UI state (the Console's send slots keep local state the Mixer has no
   access to; shared send state is 3.x territory).
3. **Delay character prints.** The strip window is narrow: Tape echo →
   TAPE, Analogue → ANALOG, Digital → DIGITAL — all measured to fit
   without overflow at every width.
4. **Return header facts.** "Input: n sends" and "Chain: In circuit /
   Bypassed" stand where a track prints Role and Tier (a Return has no
   tier, 2.7).
5. **Bus header facts.** "Output → Master" and "Peak protection: Not
   engaged" stand where a track prints Role and Tier.
6. **Bus fixtures** are gentle "glue" a MIX run would plausibly have left
   on a mix bus; all Auto (nothing hand-set on the bus yet).
7. **Peak-protection wording.** The status text "not engaged (the mix
   leaves headroom)" is fixture text; the tooltip adds "no controls in
   this build".

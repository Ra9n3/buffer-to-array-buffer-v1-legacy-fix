## Summary

* **`src/components/hardware/EngravedLabel.svelte`** — the lettering
  machined into the panel. One element (`span` by default; `p`, `div`,
  `h2`, `h3`, `figcaption` on request) in the label face, bold, uppercase,
  engraved tracking, filled with the secondary text tone — the same ink
  every approved component already prints. The cut is three layers: a dark
  copy of the text shifted **up** by `max(1px, 0.07em)` (the groove's upper
  wall, in shadow under the family's top-left light), the
  `--al-text-engraved-highlight` token shifted **down** by the same amount
  (the lower wall, lit), and a second copy of the text — an `aria-hidden`
  span laid exactly over the first — painted with a vertical gradient
  clipped to the glyphs so the paint is darker along the top of every
  stroke, where the upper wall shades the floor. Five sizes (`xs`…`xl`)
  are the five text-size tokens 10 / 11 / 13 / 16 / 20 px; nothing is in
  px; the depth scales with the type so a 20 px title is cut as deep, in
  proportion, as a 10 px legend. `kind="value"` keeps its unit's case,
  goes semibold, tracks less and sets tabular digits. Three tones
  (secondary / muted / primary paint). An unpainted `cut` finish uses the
  Task 1.1 engraving pair as the tokens describe it, for titles only.
* **`src/components/hardware/Lamp.svelte`** — a miniature incandescent
  lamp behind a lens in a machined bezel: the Task 1.4 switch's lamp lifted
  out as a shared component (D1.4.3), scaled from one length, `--lamp-size`
  (the glass, 10 px at 1×), and given its own housing. Four layers: a
  blurred **spill** on the panel behind everything, hung below and to the
  right; a **housing** ring under a 135° metal gradient standing on a drop
  shadow with the family's hairline of light beneath it; a dark **recess**
  round the glass; the **lens** — unlit, dark glass with a trace of its own
  colour and a pin of room light at the upper left; lit, a hot core a
  little above centre falling through the lamp body to a darker rim, the
  specular over it, a near glow round the bezel. Amber is the incandescent
  token `--al-material-lamp` inside the amber family; red is the red
  family, a step stronger (spill 0.62 vs 0.50, near glow 1.3×). Nothing
  else is a lamp colour; there is no hex colour and no cold white in it.
  On in 90 ms, off in 160 ms, no transition under reduced motion. With a
  `name` it is `role="img"` named "`<name>` lamp, lit / unlit"; without one
  it is presentation.
* **Playground** — `[data-dev-component="engraved-label"]`: the brief's
  specimens `INPUT OUTPUT PAN GAIN` at sm and xs and at 3× review
  magnification; `PAD HPF PHANTOM` over `26 dB 80 Hz 48 V` as a switch
  bank's print; `26 dB +4 −12 0` as values at sm and md; `LEAD VOCAL —
  TRACK 07` / `MASTER SECTION` / `MONITOR / TALKBACK` at md / lg / xl; the
  cut finish above the filled cut of the same words; two truncated track

  `[data-dev-component="lamp"]`: amber and red, unlit and lit at 1.6×; the

## Component API

### `EngravedLabel`

| Prop | Type | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `text` | `string` | required | The legend, printed as given; the component never rewrites it (uppercase is CSS, so the accessible text keeps its case). |
| `size` | `"xs" \| "sm" \| "md" \| "lg" \| "xl"` | `"sm"` | `--al-text-size-2xs / xs / md / lg / xl` = 10 / 11 / 13 / 16 / 20 px. |
| `kind` | `"label" \| "value"` | `"label"` | Legend: bold, uppercase, engraved tracking (.14em; .08em from md). Value: semibold, case kept, label tracking (.08em), tabular digits. |
| `tone` | `"normal" \| "muted" \| "bright"` | `"normal"` | Paint `--al-text-secondary` / `-muted` / `-primary`. |
| `finish` | `"filled" \| "cut"` | `"filled"` | Paint in the groove (the readable one); or the unpainted Task 1.1 engraved pair, for `lg` / `xl` titles only (D1.6.2). |
| `as` | `"span" \| "p" \| "div" \| "h2" \| "h3" \| "figcaption"` | `"span"` | Rendered element, for document structure. |
| `align` | `"start" \| "center" \| "end" \| null` | `null` | Makes the label a block and aligns the print in it. |
| `truncate` | `boolean` | `false` | Block, clipped with an ellipsis; `title` defaults to the text. |
| `id`, `title` | `string \| null` | `null` | Passed through. |

Hooks: `data-engraved-label`, `data-label-size`, `data-label-kind`,
`data-label-finish`. Note (D1.6.5): a filled label's `textContent` is the
text twice (the floor copy); read the first text node or the prop.

### `Lamp`

| Prop | Type | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lit` | `boolean` | `false` | On. |
| `color` | `"amber" \| "red"` | `"amber"` | Amber = engaged / active; red = warning / peak (spec §5). Nothing else. |
| `name` | `string \| null` | `null` | With a name: `role="img"`, `aria-label="<name> lamp, lit \| unlit"`. Without: `aria-hidden` (the parent control carries the state). |

`data-part="spill|housing|recess|lens"`.

## Rendering — exactly how each part is made

### The engraving

The label is real text; nothing is an image. Its stack, from the bottom:

1. **The paint.** `color: var(--al-text-secondary)` (`#bdb5a4`, L* 73.9),
   `font-family: var(--al-font-label)` (the condensed label stack of
   D1.1.5 — Roboto Condensed resolved in the test browser),
   `font-weight: var(--al-font-weight-bold)`, `line-height:
   var(--al-line-height-tight)` (1.15), `letter-spacing:
   var(--al-letter-spacing-engraved)` (.14em: 1.54 px at 11 px, measured),
   `text-transform: uppercase`, `white-space: nowrap`. This is the approved
   family print: in the consistency fixture the switch's own function
   print computes to `11.004px / 1.54px / 700 / rgb(189,181,164)` and the
   label beside it to `11px / 1.54px / 700 / rgb(189,181,164)`; the
   switch's value print `9.996px / 0.8px / 600 / rgb(138,131,119)` and the
   `xs` value label `10px / 0.8px / 600 / rgb(138,131,119)`.
2. **The two walls** — one `text-shadow` with two hard copies, no blur:
   `0 calc(-1 * var(--lb-depth)) 0 rgba(0,0,0,.85)` (a dark copy one depth
   **up**: the upper wall of the groove, which faces away from the
   top-left key light) and `0 var(--lb-depth) 0
   var(--al-text-engraved-highlight)` (`#4a4d45`, L* 32.2, one depth
   **down**: the lower wall, which faces the light). `--lb-depth: max(1px,
   0.07em)` — 1 px up to 14 px, 1.12 px at 16 px, 1.4 px at 20 px. This is
   the opposite direction from the approved components' `0 1px 0
   rgba(0,0,0,.9), 0 -.5px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.06)`, which shades a raised
   print (D1.6.1).
3. **The floor** — `<span class="label__floor" aria-hidden="true">{text}</span>`,
   `position: absolute; inset: 0`, `color: transparent`,
   `background-clip: text` over `linear-gradient(to bottom,
   color-mix(fill 74%, black) 0%, color-mix(fill 90%, black) 34%, fill 62%,
   color-mix(fill 94%, white) 100%)`, `text-shadow: none`, `pointer-events:
   none`, `user-select: none`. Because it is the same text in the same
   font at the same position, the gradient falls exactly on the glyphs:
   every stroke is darkest along its top edge (under the shaded wall) and
   lightest at its foot. Only rendered for `finish="filled"`.

Measured on the 3× review specimen (`m-label-zoom3-input.png`), a vertical
profile through the stem of the `I` of `INPUT` (11 px type at zoom 3, so
one depth = 3 device px): panel L* 13.7 → **upper wall L* 1.4** (3 rows) →
**paint L* 62.6 at the top of the stroke rising to 74.6 at its foot** (23
rows; the token's own L* is 73.9) → **lower wall L* 32.2** (3 rows) → panel
L* 12.9. Across the stem: 13.3 / 68.8 70.9 70.6 70.9 70.7 / 14.8. At 1×
(`m-label-sm-input.png`, same stem, anti-aliased, 1.5 px wide): 14.3 →
9.8 → 39.0 … 46.0 (8 rows, rising) → 18.0 → 12.8; fully covered glyph
pixels reach L* 74.3; the mean over all glyph pixels is 60.3. So at 1× the
cut is a one-pixel darker line above, a one-pixel lighter line below and a
+7 L* ramp down each stroke — restrained, as asked — and at 3× the three
layers are plainly separate.

**Contrast of the paint** (WCAG, from the tokens): secondary on panel
7.89 : 1, on surface 6.66 : 1, on raised plate 5.42 : 1; primary (bright)
on panel 13.28 : 1; muted on panel 4.28 : 1, on raised plate 2.94 : 1 —
the muted tone is the switch's value print and is kept for parity, but it
is below AA on the raised plate; a raised plate should carry
`tone="normal"` values (known issue below). The unpainted `cut`: engraved
fill on panel 1.14 : 1, legible only by its lit wall (1.87 : 1) — measured
on the xl specimen, 1 172 px darker than the panel by ≥ 5 L* and 421 px
lighter; the filled xl beside it, 1 887 px lighter (max L* 74.9). Hence
D1.6.2.

**Sizes** (measured computed values): xs 10 px / 1.4 px tracking; sm 11 /
1.54; md 13 / 1.04; lg 16 / 1.28; xl 20 / 1.6; values xs 0.8, sm 0.88, md
1.04. Line boxes 12 / 13 / 15 / 18 / 23 px.

**Ordinary text is not this component.** The body-text fixture sets its
paragraph in `--al-font-ui`, `--al-text-primary`, normal weight and case,
no shadow; the page copy says so.

### The lamp

Everything is a proportion of `--lp-size` (the glass): recess `max(1px,
.1×)`, ring `max(1px, .16×)`.

1. **The housing** — `padding: var(--lp-ring)` under `linear-gradient(135deg,
   color-mix(metal 78%, white) 0%, --al-material-metal 38%,
   --al-material-metal-shadow 72%, color-mix(metal-shadow 70%, black)
   100%)`: a machined ring lit at the top-left, shadowed at the bottom-right,
   like every bevel in the family. `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,.55)`
   (its seam into the panel), `0 recess calc(recess×1.5) rgba(0,0,0,.6)`
   (it stands on the panel), `0 calc(recess+1px) 0 rgba(255,255,255,.07)`
   (the family's hairline of light under a mounted part). The ring is
   padding, not a border, because Chromium snaps border widths to whole
   device pixels and a 4.8 px ring would become 4 px and shift the glass
   (D1.6.3). Measured ring L*: 67–69 at the top-left, 40 at the bottom-right
   (unlit); when lit the bottom-right rises to 49–54 — the near glow lights
   the ring from the inside.
2. **The recess** — `--al-material-recess` with `inset 0 recess
   calc(recess×1.2) rgba(0,0,0,.9)`: the dark gap between ring and glass.
   Measured L* 5–6 against the panel's 13–15.
3. **The lens, unlit** — `radial-gradient(circle at 36% 30%,
   rgba(255,255,255,.16) 0%, transparent 38%)` (a pin of reflected room
   light, upper left) over `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 58%,
   color-mix(body 22%, recess) 0%, recess 78%)` (the lamp's own colour low
   in the dark glass), `inset 0 recess calc(recess×2) rgba(0,0,0,.85)`.
   Measured: glass centre L* 26.6 `#493d2d` (amber) / 18.9 `#402824`
   (red) — a dome 12–13 L* above the panel with a visible warm or red
   cast; the pin peaks at L* 29.3 / 25.7, 1.5–3 px up-left of centre.
4. **The lens, lit** — `radial-gradient(circle at 36% 30%,
   rgba(255,255,255,.55) 0%, transparent 34%)` (the specular) over
   `radial-gradient(circle at 48% 44%, --lp-core 0%, --lp-body 42%,
   --lp-edge 92%)`, where amber's core is `color-mix(--al-function-amber-hi
   70%, white)`, body `--al-material-lamp #edb268`, edge
   `--al-function-amber-lo`; red's are the red family (`hi` 72% to white,
   `base`, `lo`). The hot spot is above centre and the glass darkens toward
   the rim, as a bulb seen through a lens does. Plus `box-shadow: inset 0 0
   1px rgba(0,0,0,.3)`, `0 0 calc(size×.2×near) calc(size×.1×near)
   color-mix(glow 70%, transparent)` and `0 0 calc(size×.6×near)
   calc(size×.2×near) color-mix(glow 32%, transparent)` — the near glow —
   with `--lp-near` 1 (amber) / 1.3 (red). Measured at 16 px glass: amber
   peak L* 92.1 `#fae6c7` at (−0.5, −1.5) px from centre, centre 87.5, rim
   45–50; red peak 81.3 `#f0bfb2`, centre 70.7, rim 29–30. At 30 px: amber
   92.4 / 88.2 / 43–47; red 81.5 / 73.5 / 26–29. At 10 px (the cluster):
   amber peak 91.5, rim 44 / 75.
5. **The spill** — a `span` behind the housing (`z-index` 0 under 1),
   `inset: -70% -80% -90% -60%` (so its box hangs 10% of the lamp below and
   10% to the right of it), `radial-gradient(ellipse at 50% 50%, glow 0%,
   transparent 68%)`, `filter: blur(calc(size × .25))`, `opacity` 0 →
   `--lp-spill-opacity` .50 (amber) / .62 (red) when lit. It is the light
   on the panel, not on the bezel. Measured as lit-minus-unlit of
   otherwise identical fixtures (so the panel's own key-light gradient
   cancels), ΔL* on the panel beyond the housing's edge and the distance
   from the lamp's centre at which the spill drops under 2 L* (the panel's
   brushed grain is ±1.5 L*):

   | glass | colour | +2 px (up-left / down-right) | +6 px | +12 px | +20 px | reach, px from centre: left / right / up / down |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 16 px | amber | +14.5 / +22.6 | +9.1 / +19.6 | +2.6 / +9.5 | 0 / +3.0 | 24 / 31 / 28 / 33 (housing radius 12.2) |
   | 16 px | red | +7.8 / +14.2 | +5.5 / +11.4 | +1.5 / +5.7 | 0 / +1.6 | 22 / 27 / 26 / 29 |
   | 30 px | amber | +19.1 / +17.3 | +15.5 / +21.5 | +7.0 / +15.8 | +4.9 / +12.9 | 51 / 61 / 47 / 57 (housing radius 22.8) |
   | 30 px | red | +9.9 / +10.6 | +7.7 / +12.6 | +3.8 / +9.5 | +1.4 / +6.6 | 41 / 53 / 45 / 53 |

   So the glow reaches about one to one-and-a-half glass diameters beyond
   the bezel, further below and to the right than above and to the left,
   and the red lamp's spill is wider while its glass is darker (its family
   is darker) — red reads as red, not as a brighter amber.

**Lit against unlit without colour:** glass centre ΔL* +61 (amber) / +52
(red) at 16 px, and the lit lamp adds a glow and a spill that the unlit one
does not have. The ring, recess and dark glass make the unlit lamp a
visible object (ring L* 67 / 40, recess 5, glass 27 against panel 13).

**Motion:** lens `transition: background 90ms, box-shadow 90ms` when lit,
160 ms when going out; spill opacity the same; `@media
(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` → `transition: none`. Measured computed
`transitionDuration` on the unlit lens: `0.16s, 0.16s`; under emulated
reduced motion `0s`. (The 90 ms on-transition is in the source and was
not timed.)

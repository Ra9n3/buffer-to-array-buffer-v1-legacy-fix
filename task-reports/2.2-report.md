## Rendering — exactly how each requirement is met

### Panel, materials, bevels, recesses (match the approved bridge)

* **Face:** `background-color: var(--al-material-panel)` with the bridge's
  two overlays — a radial key light from the top-left (`140% 70% at 22% 0%`,
  white 0.06 → black 0.20) and the same 2 px brushed grain
  (`repeating-linear-gradient(92deg, …0.012…)`).
* **Module bevel and seam:** inset `0 1px 0 var(--al-bevel-light)`, inset
  `1px 0 0` white 0.07 (lit top and left), inset `0 −1px 0
  var(--al-bevel-shadow)`, inset `−1px 0 0` black 0.45 (shadowed bottom and
  right), then `0 0 0 1px` black 0.62 and a 1–2 px drop — the dark seam
  between neighbouring modules on the rail.
* **Section divisions:** every section after the head has a 1 px
  `var(--al-bevel-shadow)` top border with an inset white 0.045 line under
  it — the engraved groove with its lit lower wall, the same light as the
  bridge's engraved legends.
* **Sends bay:** `color-mix(recess 72%, panel)` fill, inset `0 2px 3px`
  black 0.6 + inset 1 px `var(--al-border-strong)` ring + a lit 1 px lower
  lip — the bridge's recessed-well recipe.
* **Rail:** the tracks region is `color-mix(recess 55%, panel)` with inset
  `0 2px 4px` black 0.55 and a strong 1 px ring, so the modules stand proud
  of a darker channel; thin scrollbar in `metal-shadow` on `recess` (the
  bridge's channel-meter scroller colours).
* **Housing:** cheek width 0.875rem, radius 4 px, the same wood
  `color-mix` streaks (93deg 1/3/4/7 px), deck padding `xs md sm`, the
  `--b-trim` line under the top bevel, the same inset shadows — all values
  copied from the bridge CSS. Measured: bridge and bank share `x = 16` and
  width (1888 / 1248 / 992 / 736 / 340) at every viewport, 16 px apart.
* **No screw heads** anywhere (D2.1.9; both guards enforce it).

### Identity head (plan §3 "name label"; spec §7 org marker)

Number "01" engraved `sm` bright at the left, "TRK" `xs` muted at the right
(baseline-aligned), then the name row: a **0.375 × 1rem (6 × 16 px) tab** —
`background-color: var(--org)` under a glossy top-to-bottom plastic
gradient, a 1 px dark seam and a slot shadow — and the name engraved `sm`
`kind="value"`, truncating with an ellipsis ("Snare Bottom", "Rhythm Gtr L"
fit; nothing wraps). The colour never touches the panel (guard: `var(--org)`
appears exactly once, on `.strip__tab`). Seven org swatches plus neutral
are used across the twenty fixtures (drums 2, bass 1, guitars 5, keys 6,
vocals 8, harmonies 4, percussion 3, FX neutral).

### TRIM / PAN / sends (spec §5 colour families; plan §8a)

The approved Knob at 40 px: cream for trim, blue-grey for pan, green for the
two sends. Labels are the Knob's own engraved `xs` uppercase label; value
windows are its own readout button (click to type, as approved). Formatters
come from the fixture file: `formatSignedDb` ("+3.5 dB"), `formatPan`
("L 35" / "C" / "R 100"), `formatSend` ("OFF" at −60, otherwise "−18 dB").
Unity / centre sit under the knob's reference mark because the ranges are
symmetric. The two sends share one bay (`role="group"`, "Sends, Kick") so
they read as one auxiliary group.

### LEVEL (plan §3 "fader"; the anchor)

`<Fader label="Level" value={track.level} />` — the approved Fader with its
approved defaults: 72 px wide, 160 px travel, console taper, scale numerals,
readout below. Fixture positions vary from +1.5 dB (15 Lead Vocal) down to
−60 dB (20 Riser FX, muted); the rest sit between −1 and −16 dB. Nothing of the fader is
restyled; the prompt's "finger-hollow / shaft" wording is overridden by the
approved D1.3.6 cap (see Deviations).

### MUTE · SOLO · PEAK

`TactileSwitch variant="panel"` twice — function print above a plain cap in
its well, the switch's own lamp to the right of the cap: MUTE red, SOLO
amber (`engaged` from the fixture, toggled locally on press). PEAK is the
approved `Lamp` (`color="red"`, 0.75rem glass, `name` → `role="img"` "Peak,
Kick lamp, lit"), with "Peak" engraved above it exactly on the switches'
print line (measured: all three legends at y 1104.7 at 1280) and the lamp
centred on the caps' line (lamp centre 1137.7 vs cap centre 1138.8). Order
MUTE · SOLO · PEAK (owner's wording). Fixtures: mutes 09 and 16, solo 15,
peaks 01 and 12.

### Bank layout and scrolling (acceptance: 20 tracks, scroll, pinned, no overflow)

* The rail is `display: flex; width: max-content; gap: 4px`; every strip is
  `flex: 0 0 10rem` with `min-width` and `max-width` pinned.
* The tracks region is the only element with `overflow-x: auto`
  (`overscroll-behavior-x: contain`, `overflow-y: hidden`); it is a
  `role="region"` with `tabindex="0"` and a visible focus ring, so the
  keyboard reaches it (ArrowRight scrolls it 40 px, measured).
* The pinned `aside` follows the scroller in the same flex row, `flex: 0 0
  auto`, so it never scrolls away; below 40rem the Phase 0 column mode is
  kept (pinned below the rail, scrolling on its own).
* The room's grid (D2.1.7) is unchanged: bridge row `max-content`, bank row
  `minmax(max-content, 1fr)`, `overflow-y: auto` inside the room,
  `overflow-x: hidden`.

## Deviations, known issues, remaining work

**Deviations from the prompt text (all recorded in the decisions log):**

1. **Strip height 654.4 px, not ≈ 630** (D2.2.2). The ≈ 630 was my estimate
   when asking; it under-counted the section grooves, bay padding and face
   padding (≈ 24 px in total). The controls are at exactly their approved
   sizes and the paddings are at the smallest spacing tokens; the only way
   down is shrinking an approved component, which the owner ruled out. The
   room scrolls 166 px at 1920 × 1080 and 346 px at 1280 × 900 (estimates
   were ≈ 140 / ≈ 320).
2. **Fader "finger-hollow / shaft" wording** in the prompt: not implemented.
   The approved Fader (D1.3.6: the owner's ribbed 25 × 32 cream cap, no
   shaft, no finger-hollow) is used exactly as approved; re-introducing
   those rejected features would reopen an approved decision.
3. **"Adjacent lamp" for MUTE / SOLO:** satisfied by the TactileSwitch's own
   engaged lamp (Task 1.4 standing note); no second lamp is added.
4. **No screw heads** although spec §4 / plan §4 permit them — D2.1.9.
5. **Zone 02's Phase 0 header / frame removed** rather than kept around the
   bank — the owner's choice when asked (D2.2.5); the zone's `h2` id and
   accessible name are preserved by the bank's engraved legend, as the
   bridge does for zone 01.
6. **Guard lists edited** (D2.2.7): four existing guards needed their Phase
   0 / Phase 1 assumptions updated for a skinned bank and a resizable knob.
   Each edit is a single line or a replaced assertion, listed under Files.

**Known issues:**

* **Micro numerals at 40 px knobs.** The Knob's extreme numerals are drawn
  at 26 of 400 artwork units — 4.3 px at a 40 px dial — and the scale
  ticks are 1 px. The value window carries the reading, and the owner
  accepted ≈ 40 px knobs knowing this (verified legible: label and readout
  are at their full type sizes; the extremes read as marks, not text). A
  "compact" knob variant with larger extremes would be a Knob change and
  is out of scope.
* **Vertical room scroll on every tested viewport** (166 px even at
  1920 × 1080). Owner-accepted (D2.2.2). The bank's horizontal scrollbar sits
  at the foot of the rail, 654 px below the strips' heads, so on short
  windows the user scrolls the room before seeing it; the rail still
  scrolls under a horizontal wheel / trackpad gesture anywhere over it.
* **"PEAK" and "MUTE/SOLO" legends differ by 1 px in height** (EngravedLabel
  `xs` 11.5 px vs the switch's own print 12.6 px); tops are aligned.
* **The pinned placeholders stretch to the row height** (638 px) as they
  did in Phase 0 (`align-items: stretch`); their width, gap, colour and
  markup are unchanged. Task 2.4 replaces them.
* **The bridge's channel meters show 01–08** while the bank shows 01–20 —
  the 2.1 fixture, unchanged, as approved.

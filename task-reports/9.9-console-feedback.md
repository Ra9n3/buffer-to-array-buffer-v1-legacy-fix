## 3. The print's growth, precisely — and the card's hard wall

The task's example formula for the radii (`min(1.35, winH/68 × 1.35)`) cannot be taken
literally, and the measurement shows why: the 1× print's stack over the pivot — arc
(46) + major tick (5) + numeral ring (55.5, the −3 numeral nearest straight up is the
binding one) — already spends the card's whole 61-unit height budget (PIVOT_Y − FACE_Y).
At 1.35 × the radii the arc itself would stand 62.1 units over the pivot — through the
bezel — and the −3 numeral 14 units off the card. So the print scale is capped twice:
the task's 1.35 for everything with room (the numerals, the legend), and a measured
fit bound (1.02) for the radii, applied inside `polar`/`arc` so the calls keep naming
the approved constants and the 1× face is exactly itself. What the eye reads as "the
content grew": the numbers +43% and the name +36% rendered against 9.8, the arc +13%
with the taller row, the needle — growing with none of it — proportionally the smaller
blade the owner asked for. Nothing touches the bezel (measured: the −3 numeral's top
clears the card's top edge; the VLM zoom pass calls the top clearance "comfortable").

The sweep cap (±57°), the lamp/vignette centring (`translate((SW − W)/2)`), the position
law and the needle geometry are all unchanged from 9.8.

## Summary

One component, `src/components/hardware/TactileSwitch.svelte`: a native
`<button type="button" aria-pressed>` rendered with layered CSS gradients and
shadows, scaled as a whole from one host-settable length (`--switch-size`,
1.75rem = 28 px at 1×).

* **Variant A — cap-labelled (`variant="cap"`).** A 28 × 28 cap with 4.9 px
  corners (17.5% of the width; spec §2 asks 15–20%), standing 3 px off a
  dark seat on a visible side wall. Its top is a 135° gradient from the
  cream HI tone at the top-left to a shaded tone at the bottom-right, with
  a specular bloom in the upper-left on its own layer, a bright top edge, a
  shaded bottom edge, an inner turn at the bottom-right and a drop shadow.
  Measured on the 2.5× specimen along the diagonal: L 248 at the top-left,
  L 190 at the bottom-right, the wall L 106 below the face, the shadow L 14.
  The label is real text printed on the cap.
* **Variant B — panel-labelled (`variant="panel"`).** The same cap shape,
  matte: a restrained gradient between near-BASE tones, a weak sheen, a 2 px
  wall. It sits inside a recessed square well 3.5 px clear of it on each
  side — recess-token floor, a dark lip shadow at the top, a lit lower
  edge, a hairline of the strong border round the outside. The function is
  printed on the panel above the well (`PAD`) and the value below (`26dB`),
  engraved like the knob's label.
* **Pressed** — transient, pointer or key down → up: the cap sinks 2 px
  (measured 2.49 px at the top edge with the 1 px scale reduction), its
  wall shortens to 1 px, the bloom dims to 35%, an inward shadow appears
  under the seat's lip, the drop shadow tightens. The lamp does not move.
* **Engaged** — the button's `aria-pressed`, toggled by every activation:
  the lamp beside the cap lights. The cap is unchanged.
* **The lamp** — a lens in a dark bezel, 6 px to the right of the cap, level
  with its face. Off: dark glass with a trace of its colour and a pin of
  room light (centre L 63 falling to L 20 at the rim). On: a hot core, the
  lamp body, a darker lens edge, a glass specular, a near glow at the bezel
  and a blurred off-centre spill on the panel (amber: centre L 207 → L 150
  at the rim → 91 / 69 / 60 / 53 / 48 across the next 15 px of panel, against
  a panel of L 33). Amber or red only.

The knob, the fader, their fixtures, every token, every room, every strip and
the lockfile are byte-identical to `54fd227`. No dependency changed. Nothing
is committed.

## Component API

```svelte
<TactileSwitch
  variant="cap" | "panel"          required — spec §2's two conventions
  label="ON"                       cap: printed on the cap · panel: function, printed above
  value="26dB"                     panel only, optional — printed below
  lamp="amber" | "red"             default "amber" (spec §5)
  bind:engaged                     boolean, default false — the function is on; the lamp
  name="Pad 26 dB"                 optional accessible-name override
  demoState="pressed" | "focused"  playground only; default null
  onToggle={(engaged) => …}        optional callback after each activation
/>
```

Host hooks: `--switch-size` (read on the root, never declared by it);
`[data-switch]`, `data-switch-variant`, `data-switch-pressed`,
`data-switch-engaged`, `[data-switch-button]`, `[data-part=cap|cap-label|seat|well|lamp|lamp-lens|print-function|print-value]`.

Exports: `type SwitchVariant`, `type LampColour`.

**Variant structure.** One markup tree, one stylesheet; the variant is a
class on the root (`switch--cap` / `switch--panel`) that selects the seat or
the well on the button, the gloss or the matte layers on the cap, the lift
(3 px / 2 px) and the grid (one row / three rows). The label moves between
the cap (inside the button, so it is the button's name) and the panel (two
spans outside the button, joined to it by `aria-labelledby`). Nothing is
duplicated per variant.

## Rendering — exactly how each part is made

All lengths derive from `--sw-size` (= `--switch-size`); every tone is a
token or a neutral black/white alpha.

### The cap (both variants)

* A `<span class="switch__cap">`, `--sw-size` square, `border-radius: calc(--sw-size × 0.175)`.
* **Raised:** `transform: translateY(-lift)` lifts the face off its base; two
  hard box-shadows below it — `0 lift 0 0 wall` (the side wall, cream LO
  mixed 70% with black) and `0 lift+1px 0 0 foot` (LO 42% black) — extrude
  the cap's rounded shape down to the base; `0 lift+2px 3px rgba(0,0,0,.55)`
  is the drop shadow on the panel.
* **Edges:** inset shadows — a 1 px white line at 85% along the top and 45%
  down the left (facing the light), a 1 px black line at 28% along the
  bottom and 14% up the right (turning away), and an inner `-2px -3px 4px`
  black at 16% in the bottom-right corner (the convex surface rolling away).

### Variant A — glossy

* **Diagonal highlight gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, HI 0%, HI 10%,
  BASE 48%, mix(BASE 46%, LO) 100%)` — brightest top-left, darkest
  bottom-right, as spec §2 asks.
* **Specular bloom:** `::before` with `radial-gradient(62% 46% at 30% 20%,
  white .78 → .22 at 46% → 0 at 72%)` plus a faint second sheen low-right
  (`white .14 → 0`). On its own layer so `opacity` can dim it when pressed.
* **Seat:** the button itself, 1.5 px larger than the cap all round,
  recess-token fill, `inset 0 1px 1px` black 85% and a 1 px black 45% ring —
  the dark gap the cap rises from.
* **Label:** real text, `--al-font-label`, bold, uppercase, 0.04em tracking,
  `--al-text-on-cream`, a 0.5 px white 28% shadow below it (ink under the
  gloss). Size 10 px at 1×, scaling with the switch.

### Variant B — matte, in a well

* **Cap:** `linear-gradient(135deg, mix(BASE 72%, HI) 0%, BASE 52%, mix(BASE
  84%, LO) 100%)`; top edge white 42%, left 18%; the `::before` sheen is a
  single `white .20 → 0` radial; lift 2 px.
* **Well:** the button, 3.5 px larger than the cap all round, recess-token
  floor, `border-radius: cap radius + 3.5 px`; `inset 0 2px 3px` black 90%
  (the top-left lip's shadow into the well), `inset 1px 0 2px` black 55%,
  `inset 0 -1px 0` white 6% (the lit lower lip), `0 0 0 1px
  --al-border-strong` (the thin dark border round the base), `0 1px 0` white
  5% (the panel catching light under the well's edge).
* **Print:** two spans on the panel, `--al-font-label`, `--al-text-secondary`
  (function: 11 px at 1×, bold, uppercase, engraved tracking) and
  `--al-text-muted` (value: 10 px, semibold, label tracking), both with the
  knob label's engraved text-shadow (`0 1px 0` black 90%, `0 -0.5px 0` white
  6%). Centred on the well; may be wider than it without moving the lamp.

### Pressed

`.is-pressed .switch__cap`: `translateY(-1px) scale(0.965)` (2 px down, ≈1 px
narrower), wall `0 1px`, foot `0 2px`, drop shadow `0 2px 2px` black 45%, top
edge white 50%, plus `inset 0 2px 3px` black 22% — the base's lip now
shading the cap's top; `::before` opacity 0.35. Variant B: `translateY(-0.5px)`.
70 ms ease-out, none under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### The lamp

* **Bezel:** `switch__lamp`, round, lens + 1 px padding, recess fill, `inset
  0 1px 1px` black 90%, a 1 px ring of `--al-material-metal-shadow`, `0 1px
  0` white 7%. Lens diameter `0.36 × --sw-size` (10 px at 1×). Translated up
  by the lift so it is level with the resting cap's face; it never moves.
* **Lens, off:** `radial-gradient(circle at 36% 30%, white .16 → 0 at 38%)`
  over `radial-gradient(circle at 50% 58%, mix(body 22%, recess) → recess
  78%)`, `inset 0 1px 2px` black 85%.
* **Lens, on:** `radial-gradient(circle at 36% 30%, white .55 → 0 at 34%)`
  (glass specular) over `radial-gradient(circle at 48% 44%, core 0%, body
  42%, edge 92%)`; `box-shadow: 0 0 2px 1px mix(glow 70%, transparent), 0 0
  6px 2px mix(glow 32%, transparent)` (near glow).
* **Spill:** `switch__lamp::after`, an ellipse inset `-70% -80% -90% -60%`
  (larger below and to the right), `radial-gradient(ellipse at 44% 40%, glow
  → transparent 68%)`, `filter: blur(2.5px)`, opacity 0 → 0.5 when engaged.
* **Colours:** amber — core `mix(--al-function-amber-hi 70%, white)`, body
  `--al-material-lamp` (#edb268, inside spec §4a's #e8a454–#f0c27a), edge
  `--al-function-amber-lo`, glow `--al-material-lamp`. Red — core
  `mix(--al-function-red-hi 72%, white)`, body `--al-function-red-base`, edge
  `--al-function-red-lo`, glow `--al-function-red-base`.
* **Timing:** on in 90 ms, off in 160 ms (a filament's lag); none under
  reduced motion.

## Deviations, known issues, remaining work

**Deviations from the brief — none intended.** Two interpretations are worth
stating:

* The cap colour is not in the spec; the owner chose cream before any code
  was written (D1.4.1).
* "Reduce apparent size by 1–2 px" is done with `scale(0.965)` on a 28 px
  cap (≈1 px) together with the 2 px sink, rather than a larger scale that
  would read as the cap shrinking instead of sinking.

**Not built, by scope:** a `disabled` state (the brief asks for default,
pressed and engaged); function-coloured caps (one `family` prop away — the
cap's three custom properties are the only thing to vary); a lamp-position
option; the separate reusable `Lamp` component (Task 1.6 — the switch's lamp
is self-contained so it can be lifted out).

**Known limitations:**

* The lamp's spill and the cap's drop shadow are drawn with `box-shadow` and
  a blurred pseudo-element, so a host with `overflow: hidden` tight to the
  switch will clip them; the component's own box includes the raised cap but
  not the glow.
* A value label much wider than the well (e.g. `PHANTOM`) overflows the
  component's box on both sides by design, so it stays centred under the
  switch and does not move the lamp; in a strip narrower than the print it
  would meet the neighbour's print.
* Holding Enter repeats activation at the key-repeat rate — the platform's
  button behaviour, left as is.
* At 1× the eight-pixel-tall print and the 10 px lens are at the lower limit
  of what the panel's engraved style can carry; they are crisp from ≈1.5× up.

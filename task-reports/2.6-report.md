  edge, no labels.

  row's tab colour for all 53 clips at every width.

### Waveform / clip placeholders
- Static, locally generated: a seeded hash and sixteen recipes; identical
  on every load; nothing read from audio.
- Distinguishable at a glance (see screenshots): kick = sparse decaying
  transients; snare = backbeat hits (bottom lighter); hat = dense 8ths with
  accents; overheads = cymbal wash with crash swells at section starts;
  room = diffuse wash with a kick thump; bass = quarter notes with phrase
  rests; rhythm guitars = dense sustained blocks; lead = sparse chorus
  fills, a continuous vibrato line in the Solo; piano = stereo chords
  ringing into the off-beats; pad = slow stereo swells; vocal = phrases
  with breaths between; harmonies lighter; shaker = fine 16ths; riser =
  stereo swell into each chorus and the solo.
- Boundaries and positions clear: a plate with a 1 px dark edge, 4 px
  inset top and bottom, 1 px seam between touching clips, edges on bar
  lines; tooltip with the bar range.
